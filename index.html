FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Tilt hold my funds?

No. Tilt builds the trade, your wallet signs it, and it settles on Solana straight back to your wallet. Deposits go into Jupiter Lend vaults in your name. Tilt never has custody.

Where do trades go?

Every buy and sell is routed by Jupiter, which splits the order across Solana markets for the best price. The fee Jupiter charges is shown before you approve.

How does Earn pay?

Jupiter Lend lends your deposit to borrowers and passes their interest to depositors. The APY shown is the live rate and changes as borrowing demand changes. It is not guaranteed.

Can I withdraw any time?

Yes, as long as the vault has free liquidity. When a vault is heavily borrowed, a large withdrawal may have to wait until borrowers repay.

When do leveraged longs and shorts arrive?

They're next. They need a lending program for memecoins with sound liquidations, and we'll only switch them on once that's safe. Until then the calculator shows how they'll work.

What do I need to start?

A Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, a little SOL for network fees, and USDC or SOL to trade with.

Which way are you leaning?

Connect a Solana wallet and make your first trade in under a minute.
